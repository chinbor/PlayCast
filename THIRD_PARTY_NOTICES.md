The Douyin connector references local dycast and DouyinLiveWebFetcher implementations for protocol facts and signing behavior. Those repositories are not part of this repository or installer. DouyinLiveWebFetcher is AGPL-3.0; no repository-level license was present in the local dycast checkout reviewed on 2026-10-02. Reference lineage is documented in electron/douyin-signing.cts and electron/douyin-extensions.cts. The signing implementation's authorship and permission for any expressive adaptation remain unresolved. Attribution alone does not grant a right to relicense upstream work; do not describe the entire release as cleared MIT-only until this is resolved.
