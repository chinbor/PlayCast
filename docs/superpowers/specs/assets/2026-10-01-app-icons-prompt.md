## 生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: original Windows desktop application icon family concept sheet for Live Interaction Tool, a playful live-stream interaction utility with separate challenge and chat display applications. Create ONE clean concept sheet on a plain warm off-white background. Exactly THREE equally sized standalone app icons in a single horizontal row, evenly spaced, with abundant clear margin. No captions or any text anywhere. Each icon has the same softly rounded square silhouette, the same padding and optical weight, and a subtle tactile edge, face-on without perspective. Favor bold, friendly, very simple shapes, restrained soft shading, and clear negative space that stays readable at 24 pixels. Left icon: coral-red tile with a large warm ivory play triangle and two simple curved broadcast strokes, expressing the main live interaction app. Center icon: warm amber-gold tile with one bold warm ivory trophy silhouette, expressing the challenge display. Right icon: muted turquoise-teal tile with two bold overlapping warm ivory conversation bubbles, expressing the message display. The three central silhouettes must be unmistakably different even in grayscale. Refined contemporary desktop utility identity, cheerful but not childish. Avoid intricate detail, little badges, sparkles, thin outlines, neon glow, cyberpunk styling, game scenery, lettering, numbers, watermarks, mockup devices, third-party logos, Douyin/TikTok logos, Riot/League of Legends logos. This is an icon design sheet, not a screenshot of an application UI.
